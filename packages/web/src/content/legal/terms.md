## Every figure is an estimate

The numbers come from two places: official open data, and what you type in.

- Sold prices come from HM Land Registry Price Paid Data.
- Floor areas come from EPC records.
- The index used to move a past price to today is the UK House Price Index.
- Area statistics come from ONS, the official deprivation indices, police.uk,
  the Environment Agency and Natural Resources Wales.
- Stamp duty and land transaction tax come from the published rates.

None of that is a valuation, and none of it knows your property. Sold prices
are what somebody else paid for somewhere else. If you change an assumption,
the answer changes — that is the point of it.

**The data has a lag.** Land Registry sold prices are published in arrears and
the site shows the as-of date of the data it is using. A recent sale may not be
there yet.

## England and Wales only

The sold-price and area data covers England and Wales. Postcodes in Scotland
and Northern Ireland are refused rather than guessed at. The stamp duty tool
covers Stamp Duty Land Tax (England and Northern Ireland) and Land Transaction
Tax (Wales); it does not cover Scotland's LBTT.
