6-Bedroom Home For Sale in Carlton Terrace, Swansea

A spacious and recently renovated 6 bedroom home situated in the sought-after Carlton Terrace area of Swansea, offering a fantastic combination of period character, modern finishes and versatile accommodation across multiple floors.

Arranged over basement, ground, first and second floors, the property provides generous living space and would make an excellent family home or investment opportunity as it has a HMO licence.

The ground floor offers flexible living and entertaining space, together with an open-plan kitchen and dining area ideal for family meals and social gatherings. Additional facilities include a utility room, bathroom and cloakroom, adding further practicality to the accommodation.

To the upper floors are four generously sized en-suite bedrooms, each providing a comfortable and private space. The property also benefits from several storage rooms, offering plenty of useful additional space.

Externally, the rear garden provides a private area for outdoor entertaining, relaxing or enjoying the warmer months.

The property has been recently renovated, with updated fixtures and fittings throughout while retaining the character and appeal of the original building.

A particular highlight is the provision of two large garages with rear-lane access, offering excellent parking, storage or potential additional use.

Located in Swansea, Carlton Terrace provides convenient access to a wide range of local amenities, schools, public transport links and the city centre. The property's generous bedroom accommodation and location also make it an attractive option for investors looking to appeal to students or professional tenants.
